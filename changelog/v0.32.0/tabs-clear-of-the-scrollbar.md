---
title: A profile's tabs stay clear of the scrollbar
type: fix
area: players
impact: minor
---
The bar of tabs on a profile — Overview, Activity, Collection, Replays — is now
part of the page's frame rather than something the page scrolls past. The
scrollbar starts underneath it, so the bar runs the full width of the window
and reads as one piece of chrome with the top bar above it, and the page's left
and right margins are finally the same width.
