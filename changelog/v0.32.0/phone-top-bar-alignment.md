---
title: The top bar sits square with the menu on phones
type: fix
area: site
impact: minor
---
On a phone the UAR mark now lines up with the menu button and the icons down
the left edge, instead of standing further in than either of them, and the two
ends of the bar are evenly spaced — the account and search buttons sit as far
from the right edge as the mark does from the left. The rule under the bar also
runs the whole way across again; it used to stop where the menu strip begins.
