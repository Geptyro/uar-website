---
title: The upload button is gone from the top bar on phones and tablets
type: improvement
area: replays
impact: minor
---
Replays live on the machine you play on, so the upload button in the top bar
now only appears on devices that could actually have an `.SC2Replay` file
sitting on them. Nothing is lost either way — the upload form on
[Replays](/replays) still takes a file from anywhere.
