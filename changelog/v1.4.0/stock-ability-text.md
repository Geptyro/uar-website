---
title: Stock abilities have their text and icon back
type: fix
area: wiki
---
Abilities a class carries straight from the base game, like the AMX's Siege
Mode, showed no description and no icon. Ninety-odd map abilities whose text
sits on the button rather than the ability (Jet, Binoculars, Claymore, the
Flare Gun) were blank as well. All of them read their button now.
