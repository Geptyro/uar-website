---
title: Older replay files are released once everyone has a newer game
type: improvement
area: replays
impact: minor
---

Replay files are kept as a progression backup — if you ever lose your UAR
bank, you can download your most recent replay and recover from it. Only your
latest game is needed for that, so once every player in an older game has
recorded a more recent replay of their own, that file is released and the
space reclaimed.

Your profile now has a **Download latest replay** button, next to Last seen —
that is the one to hold on to if you ever need to restore your progression,
and it is always kept.

Nothing else changes: older games stay on record with their full player list,
XP, prestige and classes, and still count towards every leaderboard and
profile. Only the downloadable file goes, and both the replay list and the
replay page mark when that has happened. Uploading a game we have already
processed is still recognised as a duplicate, whether or not we kept the file.
