---
title: Boards everywhere now draw the picture at the height of its row
type: improvement
area: site
---
Played with, Classes played and the replay history on a player profile now draw
the portrait or the class picture at the full height of its row, so a table
reads down as a column of faces instead of a column of small stamps.

The same board is now used everywhere it belongs:

- XP gained and Class picks on the front page were rebuilt on it, position
  numbers included.
- Top players on a class page moved out of the narrow side rail and into the
  main column, where the whole row finally fits instead of being cut off.
- Item and armament tables on a class page draw their pictures the same way,
  and Handling moved into the side rail beside them.

Section headings on a profile also stand clear of what is above them again —
every one of them but the first had been pinned to the tight spacing meant only
for the heading that opens the page.
