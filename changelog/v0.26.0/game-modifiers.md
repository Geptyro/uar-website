---
title: Modifiers now show alongside the game mode
type: feature
area: replays
---
Outbreak, 1 life, Tier 1, Rifle, Blizzard, Sushis — the modifiers a lobby
votes on top of its mode are now read out of the replay and shown next to the
mode everywhere games appear: the overview's **Last games**, the replay
archive, your **Replay history**, and each game's own page.

Each has its own icon and colour, and hovering one tells you what it does in
the game's own words. The colours group them: warm for the ones that make the
fight harder, cool for the ones that restrict what you may bring, and grey for
the training-mode options, since a training game earns no XP and saves nothing.

Like the mode, the modifier vote happens after the lobby, so it is only
readable from a recording that lasted past it — and unlike the mode, no
save-file counter can fill in the gaps afterwards. Games from before this
change keep their modifiers wherever their replay file is still stored.
