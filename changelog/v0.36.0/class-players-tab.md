---
title: 'Players tab on every class: totals, win rate by mode, top 25 by time, last games'
type: feature
area: players
---
Each class's **Players** tab shows how many players have been recorded on it,
how many games, how long it has been in play, the average game length, and how
often those games were won — with the same breakdown per mode. Under it, the
twenty-five players with the most recorded time on the class, each with their
games and their won–lost on it; the **last 7 days** on the class — games,
players, hours, win rate and the week's top five, live; games per week over
the last six months; the classes most often seen in the same game as it; and
the last games it was seen in, linked to their replay pages. The all-time
totals fill in with the next recorded game after this release.
