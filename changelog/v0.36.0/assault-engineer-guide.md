The second class guide, on the Assault Engineer's **Guide** tab. It opens on
the Predator — getting in, its guns and every ability with its cost and
cooldown, and what happens when it breaks (it never dies; you overhaul it) —
then Engineering: repair, refit, overhaul and reconstruct, the armor and
battery upgrades, and a table of exactly what **Craft** does to each thing it
can touch, from a scope to the City Guard. Plus the two City Guard add-ons only
this class can buy and the Predator's unlock ladder. Numbers read from the map
file.
