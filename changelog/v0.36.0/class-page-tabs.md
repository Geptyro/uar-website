---
title: Class pages split into tabs — Overview, Gear, Players, and a Guide where one is written
type: feature
area: wiki
impact: major
---
A class page was one long column that answered everything at once. It is now
the same frame a player profile has: **Overview** for what the class does (its
skill trees, its commands, how its weapon handles, what it is issued, its
panel), **Gear** for everything it can carry — issued weapons beside every
pickup, then armor, equipment and consumables at full width — and **Players**
for who plays it. The tabs are keyboard-driven the way a profile's are, and each
one is its own URL.

The Predator is no longer listed as a class of its own: it is the
**Assault Engineer's** vehicle tab, with its weapons, its abilities, its
handling, its gear and the ladder of chassis upgrades. The old address sends
you there.
