---
title: The phantom margin on a phone is gone
type: fix
area: site
---
On a narrow phone the overview and the class pages could still be dragged a
little sideways, leaving a blank strip down the right and along the bottom. An
invisible table — the one that reads the activity and jam charts out to screen
readers — was sizing itself to its contents and hanging off the page. It now
stays where it belongs, and the page holds still at any width.
