---
title: "Fixed: the Assault Engineer's page promised Barricade, Satchel Charge and a Repair Drone"
type: fix
area: wiki
---
The Engineering Skills tree is shared by both engineers, and so is its in-game
tooltip — which lists the Combat Engineer's unlocks: Arc Cell and Flash at
level 2, Barricade at 3, Satchel Charge at 4. The Assault Engineer levels the
same tree but never has any of those abilities, so his class page was promising
gear he cannot get.

His Engineering Skills card now lists what the levels really open for him,
read from the abilities' own requirements: Repair, Disarm, Overhaul and
Reconstruct at level 1, Armor Upgrade and Refit at 2, Battery Upgrade at 3,
Craft at 4 — with a note on why the in-game text says otherwise.

The Repair Drone is gone from his ability list too: its button needs Security
level 2, a tree only the Combat Engineer can level, so on the Assault Engineer
it could never light up. The same check now applies to every class — an ability
whose only gate is a tree the class cannot level is not listed.
