---
title: Long-standing players' profiles open quickly
type: improvement
area: players
---
A profile sent every game you had ever played to your browser and then showed
fifty of them — so the more you played, the slower your own page got. The most
active player's profile was taking nearly four seconds.

Only the page of history you are looking at is fetched now, so a profile opens
in about a third of a second whether you have played ten games or four hundred,
and stays that way as you play more.
