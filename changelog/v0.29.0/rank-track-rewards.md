---
title: What each rank track is actually worth
type: feature
area: wiki
---
Picking Enlisted, Warrant or Commissioned is not just a different ladder of names —
every rank quietly stacks a hidden buff on your hero, and Warrant and Commissioned
both start **five stacks ahead** of a fresh Private. [The ranks page](/ranks) now
shows the running total at every rank: move speed, ranged damage, and the max life
each stack costs you.

Three ranks also hand a class something outright, and those are marked too:

- **Command Sergeant Major** — Frag Grenade for the Combat Medic, Expert Marksman for the Rifleman
- **Chief Warrant Officer 5** — the Flare Gun, for both
- **Major General** — a Rifleman or Combat Medic spawns with a free mini soldier

The class pages carry the same information: the infobox panel is now "Rank tracks",
and each track shows what it eventually gives you alongside what it costs to unlock.
