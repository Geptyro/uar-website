---
title: Class life and energy now match what you see in game
type: fix
area: wiki
---
Every class spawns with one point of Fitness, Dexterity and Mechanical, and those
points are worth **+8 life** and **+15 energy** before you have spent a single skill
point. The class pages were showing the bare template numbers instead, so a Combat
Medic read 420 life and 190 energy where the game shows 428 and 205.

Life, energy and health regeneration are now the values you actually see when your
hero drops in. The tank and the APC are unchanged — vehicles have no attributes.
