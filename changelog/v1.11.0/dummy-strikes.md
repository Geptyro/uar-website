title: Orbital Strike damage, and buffs on dropped strikes
type: fix

Orbital Strike now shows its 600 damage. Some strikes are dealt by a unit the map drops on the target, not by you: Orbital Strike, Call for CAS and the shells. Your buffs don't reach those hits, except Whiz Kid, which the map copies over. The Orbital Strike card says so now. Whiz Kid is no longer listed as something Call for CAS puts on you.
