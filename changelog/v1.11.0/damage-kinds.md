---
title: Damage kinds, and what each buff raises
type: feature
area: wiki
impact: major
---
Every hit on the site now says its damage kind: ranged, spell, splash or melee. Buffs raise one kind only, so this tells you which ones reach it. Hover the kind to see which SIs raise it.

The splash has a kind of its own, and it is often not the hit's. The Rjx-73's grenade hits its target with ranged damage and splashes the units around it with spell damage. So Whiz Kid, which raises spell damage, skips the grenade's direct hit but boosts its splash. The same goes for the Rjx-73's minigun. Whiz Kid also covers most explosives (frag grenades, rockets, Orbital Strike, the nuke), the burning field from Plasma Grenades and the Rjx-73's Railgun.

Effect pages for buffs that raise or cut a damage kind now list, class by class, the hits they reach and the ones they miss. See [Whiz Kid](/effects/WhizKid) for an example. SI buffs have their own effect pages now, linked from the SI.
