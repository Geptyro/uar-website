---
title: 'Live lobby & game status in the top bar'
type: feature
area: players
---

Players running the UAR Companion app now show up as **in a lobby** or **in
a game**: a teal chip counts open lobbies, a violet chip counts games in
progress, and hovering either one lists who is inside (with links to their
profiles). The ready-to-play list badges those players too.

Joining a lobby or starting a game withdraws your ready flag automatically
— you're already playing, no need to advertise. Ready toggling is now
rate-limited per account so the list stays calm.
