---
title: Critical shot numbers on the skills that crit
type: feature
area: wiki
---
Skill cards now show the critical shot each level gives, its chance and its multiplier, read from the map script. That covers Marksmanship on every class that has it, the Cyborg's Agility, the Prototype's Cybernetics and the FP-500 Combat Walker's Marksmanship. Soldier Skills 2 shows the 2× crit it gives the Cavalry Scout, Combat Engineer, Combat Medic, Fire Support Specialist, Javelin Gunner and Assault Engineer. Focus Fire, the Sniper's camp, the SOF-AR and Power Armor MK IV show what they add to a crit.

A few numbers differ from the in-game text. The base chance is 12%, not 12.5%, and Focus Fire adds 20% to it, not 7.5%.

In 1 life, a [Combat Medic](/mos/CombatMedic) with Soldier Skills 2 or more crits at 2× whatever their Marksmanship level. The map checks the Soldier Skills bonus after Marksmanship and the last check sets the multiplier. The card shows this under each level. The dexterity and range from both still add up.
