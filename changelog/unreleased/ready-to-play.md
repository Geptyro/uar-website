---
title: '"Ready to play" flag in the top bar'
type: feature
area: players
---
Signed-in players can flag themselves as **ready to play** from the top bar.
The flag lasts one hour — the button counts down and shifts color as time
runs out, and a restart button rewinds it to a full hour. Hovering shows
everyone currently ready, so you can see who's up for a game right now —
and changes appear live in every open window.
