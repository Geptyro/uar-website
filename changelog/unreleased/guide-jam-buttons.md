The jam step of the [quick guide](/guide) now spells out what **Z then A**
actually is: the **Additional actions** button, then **Immediate/Remedial
Action** inside it — both drawn with the icons you will be looking for on your
command card.

The icon a jam puts on screen sits above them, with arrows closing in on it, so
you know the thing before it happens to you.
