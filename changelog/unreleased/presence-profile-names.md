---
title: Ready and live lobbies now show your SC2 name, not your battletag
type: fix
area: site
---
The "ready to play" list and the lobby and game rosters in the top bar read out
Battle.net battletags, which is rarely the name anyone knows you by. They now
show the StarCraft II profile name of your main profile — the one the lobby
shows and the one on your [player page](/players) — with the battletag kept as
the tooltip.

A lobby also used to list each player who runs the Companion app twice, once by
name and once by tag. Everyone appears once now, and players the site
recognises link straight to their profile.
