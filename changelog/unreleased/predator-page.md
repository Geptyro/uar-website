---
title: The Assault Engineer's Predator has its own page
type: feature
area: wiki
---
The Predator was only ever a line in the entity list, even though it is a whole
second unit: its own life and energy, two weapon modes, and nine abilities the
Assault Engineer never has in hand. It now has a class page like any other —
[Predator](/mos/Goliath2) — and the two link to each other, so you can read the
Mecha Skills tree on one side and what it actually unlocks on the other.

The page also carries the six Predator Pilot unlocks and, at last, the **Twin
Autocannons MK II**: that weapon is Predator-only, and because the Predator was
not a class, the site had been listing it as usable by nobody. Every other
vehicle item the walker can take — scopes, shield generators, the recon drone
module — now shows up there too.

You can turn the walker around on its page too — the Black Ops Goliath it is
built from is now one of the 3D models.
