---
title: Compare weapon handling across every class
type: feature
area: wiki
---
A new **Classes compared** page (from “Compare all” above the class list) puts
magazine size, reload time, jam risk and panel hotkeys for all 26 classes in one
sortable table, with bars so the differences are obvious at a glance.

The spread is bigger than it feels in game: reload runs from 3.5s on the Prototype
to 20s on the FP-500, and per-shot jam risk varies almost eightfold between the
Alligator and the small-magazine classes. Six classes never jam at all.
