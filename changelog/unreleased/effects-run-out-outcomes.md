---
title: What Infected Bite does when it runs out
type: fix
area: wiki
---
When [Infected Bite](/effects/InfectedBite) runs its full course, it has a 33% chance to kill the hero, and a hostile Risen spawns where they fell. A cure or a revive before the timer ends removes it with no roll. The page used to say any hero who died with it rose as a Risen, which is not what the map does.

Effect pages now show this for any effect that rolls, kills or spawns when it ticks or runs out, read from the game data. Opportuned Shot, for example, rolls a 30% chance every 3 s for its attack speed bonus.
