---
title: The weekly XP board now really covers seven days
type: fix
area: players
---
The overview's "XP gained" board used to reach up to three days back past the
start of the week to find something to measure you against, so games you played
before the week even opened could land on it — one player was showing 24 games
and nearly 40% more XP than they had actually earned in the last seven days. It
now measures strictly between your first and last uploaded game inside the
window, so every row covers the same week and the ranking is comparable.

Two side effects worth knowing: totals for the same week will read a little
lower than before, and if only one of your games from the week has been
uploaded there is nothing to measure against, so you will not appear until a
second one lands.
