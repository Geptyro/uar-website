---
title: The ready list is easier to read at a glance
type: improvement
area: site
---
Each player in the ready-to-play list showed how many minutes were left on
their flag. Someone else's hour running out isn't something you can act on,
and it read like they were about to leave — which it never meant. The list now
shows simply who is waiting.

Your own remaining time still appears on your own chip, next to the button
that restarts it. The chip also now reads "Ready to play now", which is what
the flag actually claims: the next hour, not a standing preference.
