---
title: 'UAR Tray is now UAR Companion — and does much more'
type: improvement
area: replays
---

The desktop app announced last release grew past its name. **UAR Companion**
still uploads your replays automatically after each game, and now also lets
you flag yourself **ready to play** without opening the site, shows open
lobbies and running games, and tells the site when you're in a lobby or in a
game so everyone can see who's actually available.

Windows, Linux and macOS builds are linked from the top bar and the replays
page. Sign in once with Battle.net and forget about it. Already installed?
Grab the new build once — from then on it updates itself.
