---
title: A proper light mode, and yours to choose
type: feature
area: site
---
The site followed your device's light/dark setting and gave you no say in it —
and its light half was only half done, with the menu and top bar staying dark
green whichever way you were. Light mode is now a light mode throughout, and
**Account** has an **Appearance** choice: System, Light or Dark.

The settings cog that opens it sits in the top bar whether or not you have
connected a Battle.net account. Your choice is remembered in that browser and
applied before the page draws, so it never flashes the other one at you.
