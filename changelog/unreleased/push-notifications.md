---
title: Browser notifications when a lobby forms or someone is ready
type: feature
area: site
---
UAR can now notify you the way the companion app does — when a lobby opens, or
when someone raises their "ready to play" flag — except it works from any
browser, with the site closed. Turn it on per browser under
[Account](/account); each one gets its own toggles, so your phone can buzz
while your desktop stays quiet.

- You are never notified about your own flag, or about a lobby you are already
  in.
- There is a **Send a test notification** button, so you can check the phone in
  your pocket actually buzzes before trusting it to.
- A flag running out is not a notification. Only someone dropping it early is.
- On iPhone and iPad, add UAR to the Home Screen first — Apple only delivers
  notifications to installed web apps, never to a Safari tab.
