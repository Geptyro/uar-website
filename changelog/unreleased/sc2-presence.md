---
title: 'Live lobby & game status in the top bar'
type: feature
area: players
---

Players running the UAR Tray companion app now report whether they are in a
lobby or in a game, and the top bar shows it: an amber chip counts **open
lobbies** (hover to see who is inside — join them!), a blue chip counts
**games in progress**, and the ready-to-play list badges each player who is
currently in a lobby. Starting a game automatically withdraws your ready
flag (and flagging is blocked while you play); ready toggling is also
rate-limited so the list stays calm.
