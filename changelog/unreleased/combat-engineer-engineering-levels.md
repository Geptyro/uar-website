---
title: Engineering Skills now lists what the Combat Engineer really unlocks
type: fix
area: wiki
---
The in-game tooltip for Engineering Skills only names the unlocks specific to
each class, so the Combat Engineer page repeated that and left the rest out.
Level 1 also brings Overhaul and Reconstruct, and level 2 brings Refit. That is
the same repair kit an Assault Engineer gets, and Craft at level 4 is the only
part of it the Combat Engineer does not have.

The Assault Engineer guide claimed nobody else could Refit, Overhaul or
Reconstruct. It has been corrected.
