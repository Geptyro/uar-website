---
title: Guides are easier to read and to vote on
type: improvement
area: wiki
---
A guide now opens with its title, its author and the modes it is for, and the
vote is right there: two buttons, Helpful and Not really, with their counts. On
a wide screen a side column keeps the vote and the guide's sections in view
while you read. The same question waits at the end of the guide.

Signing in to vote or to comment now brings you back to the page you were on,
not to your account page.
