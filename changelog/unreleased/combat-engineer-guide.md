The first class guide, on the Combat Engineer's **Guide** tab. It opens on the
minimap with the three heaps of scraps that are on the ground at 0:00 (all 28
cases, from the map file), the City Guard, the five caves a satchel seals and
the landing zone — then walks the class's jobs in order: feeding the City Guard
and what each add-on costs, sealing the caves and what that stops, keeping the
machines running, and holding ground. The numbers are read from the map's
script.
