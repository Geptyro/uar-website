---
title: Links to the site now unfurl properly, and search engines can find it
type: improvement
area: site
---
Paste any page into Discord and you get a card with the page's name and a short
summary instead of a bare link.

Every page also describes itself now — a class page says what the class is, an
entity page quotes its in-game description, a profile says how many games the
player has — so a search for a unit or a battletag can actually land on the
right page. Your account page and the individual replay pages stay out of search
results.
