---
title: Ammo, jamming and class-panel hotkeys on every MOS page
type: feature
area: wiki
---
Class pages now document the things the game never tells you. A new **Handling**
section shows your magazine size, reload time, and exactly how likely your weapon
is to jam — plus how long clearing one takes.

There is also a small chart of how jam risk changes the longer you go without
one — it climbs steadily, which is why jams feel like they arrive in streaks.

Jam odds are not the same for everyone: the roll is tied to the magazine size
your class spawns with, so small-magazine classes jam noticeably more per shot.
The Squad Designated Marksman jams about 1.7× as often as a Rifleman, and
carrying more ammo does **not** help — a Magazine Extender doubles what you
carry without changing the odds. The risk also climbs the longer you go without
a jam, which is why they feel like they arrive in streaks.

Classes with a mini-panel (medics, engineers, Ghost, Sniper, the Robot and more)
also get a **Class panel** grid listing every button with its hotkey, including
the Robot's shift/ctrl body-part bindings.
