---
title: You stay signed in while you keep playing
type: fix
area: site
---
Signing in lasted exactly 30 days and then ended, however much you had used the
site in between. It now renews itself whenever you visit or the companion app
checks in, so only a month away actually signs you out.

The "ready to play" button also says something when it can't do what you asked
— an expired sign-in, or being in a lobby already — instead of quietly doing
nothing.
