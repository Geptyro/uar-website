---
title: Battle.net sign-ins that missed your StarCraft II profile now fix themselves
type: fix
area: site
---
If Battle.net did not send your StarCraft II profiles when you first signed in, your account page showed an error and your player page never got linked to you. The site now tries a second way during sign-in, and if that fails too, it keeps asking in the background while you browse. Once Battle.net answers, your profile links up without you having to sign in again.
