---
title: A year of activity on the front page
type: feature
area: site
---
Under the seven-day chart on the front page there is a second one covering the
last twelve months, so you can see the whole year at a glance rather than just
this week. One bar per day, under a seven-day average.

Switch it between players in game and games played, and hover any day for both.

It only knows about games that were uploaded, and plenty are missing from the
older months, so read the early part of the year as a floor rather than a
count.
