---
title: 'UAR Tray: auto-upload your replays'
type: feature
area: replays
---

New companion app for your PC: **UAR Tray** sits in the system tray, uploads
new Undead Assault Reborn replays right after each game (keeping your profile
and the leaderboards fresh), and shows how many players are flagged "ready to
play" — with an optional desktop notification when someone is. Get it for
Windows, Linux or macOS from the link on the replays page.
