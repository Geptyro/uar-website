---
title: Every unlock on your profile explains itself
type: improvement
area: players
---
Hover — or tap, on a phone — any class, Skill Identifier, medal, camo or decal
on a player profile and a card tells you what it is, how it is earned and
whether that player has it. Each card carries a way through to the page it
lives on: a class goes to its own page, the rest to their section.

Medals used to print their whole description under every icon, which made the
section twice as long as the ones around it. They are now icons like
everything else, with the text in the card. Classes and Skill Identifiers sit
side by side, as do medals and decals, all at one tile size.
