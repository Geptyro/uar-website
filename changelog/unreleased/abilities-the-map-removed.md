When the map patches a class's ability list, it does so slot by slot — and a
slot that gets overwritten or cleared takes its old ability with it. The site
was merging those lists by name instead, so it kept abilities the map had in
fact removed.

Gone from the pages, because gone from the game: Camouflage and a stray
Monomolecular Wire ability card on the Squad Designated Marksman (the
Monomolecular Wire skill tree stays — that is the one the map kept); Prone on
the Reaper, the Rjx-73, the Sushiman, the Combat Walker and the Alligator; Jump
on the Combat Walker and the Alligator.
