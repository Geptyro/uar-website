---
title: The site works on a phone
type: improvement
area: site
---
The menu is now behind a burger button at every screen size, and collapses to
a strip of icons rather than disappearing — every destination stays one click
away while the page gets the width back. The site remembers how you left it.

On a phone the menu keeps that icon strip in place and opens the full panel
over the page, the top bar takes a second row for the page title, and the
lobby, game and ready chips shrink to their icon and count so the first row
stays on one line.

- **Fix** — tapping a top-bar chip on a phone opened its player list half off
  the screen. Pops and tooltips now flip and slide to stay fully visible.
- The site version moved out of the top bar into the menu, above **Overview**,
  with a small dot when there is a release you have not read yet.
