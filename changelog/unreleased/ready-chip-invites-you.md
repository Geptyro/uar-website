---
title: The ready-to-play chip tells you when people are waiting
type: fix
area: site
---
When other players flagged themselves as ready, signed-out visitors saw an
amber chip inviting them in — but if you were signed in and had not flagged
yourself, the chip stayed plain. The one person who could actually join was
the one not being told.

Now anyone who has not flagged themselves sees the amber chip with the number
waiting. Your own chip keeps its green-to-red countdown once you are flagged,
so the two never get confused.
