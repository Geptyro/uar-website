---
title: 'Uploads no longer slow the site down'
type: improvement
area: replays
---

Reading a replay is heavy work, and until now the site did it in the middle
of serving pages — so while the companion app uploaded a game, everyone
else's browsing stuttered. Replays are now read alongside the site instead
of in front of it: a long game that used to freeze page loads for a quarter
of a second barely registers, and a companion working through a backlog of
hundreds of games no longer makes the site crawl.
