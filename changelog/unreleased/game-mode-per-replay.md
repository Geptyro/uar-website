---
title: Every game now shows which mode it was played on
type: feature
area: replays
impact: major
---
Recruit, Nightmare, Survival, Apocalypse — the mode a game was voted onto is
now read out of the replay and shown everywhere games are listed: the
**Last games** card on the overview, the replay archive, your **Replay
history** on your profile, and each game's own page.

Each mode has its own icon and colour. The six difficulties run green through
red in order, so a run of games reads as a difficulty curve at a glance, and
the side modes — Competitive, Survival, PMC, Invasion, Infested, PMC+ — carry
their own cool colours so they never look like a rung on that ladder.

StarCraft records the lobby, not the vote that happens after it, so the mode
has to be reconstructed: from the vote itself in the replay, and from the
per-mode win counters in every player's save file. The two are checked against
each other and agree on 170 of the 171 games where both can answer.

A game shows no mode when neither can — a replay uploaded by someone who left
during the vote, of a game that was lost. Upload a later game and won ones
fill themselves in.
