---
title: Replays from before a lost save are kept for good
type: improvement
area: replays
---
Your replay is your bank backup, but the old rule only kept your most recent
one — so if you changed computer or lost your save, the last recording of the
progression you lost was eventually deleted, while the fresh-bank games that
replaced it were kept.

Now, whenever a profile's career XP is recorded going backwards, the replay
holding the bank from just before is kept permanently. Every such point is
kept, not just the most recent, so a profile wiped more than once keeps a
replay from before each one.

This only protects losses from here on — replays already deleted cannot come
back.
