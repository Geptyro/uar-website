---
title: 'Upload your whole replay history'
type: improvement
area: replays
---

The upload limit that capped you at a handful of replays per hour is gone.
Install the companion app with hundreds of past games sitting in your
StarCraft II folder and it will now work through all of them, one after
another, in minutes rather than days — every game you have ever played
lands on your profile and in the leaderboards.
