---
title: 'One lobby shows up as one lobby'
type: fix
area: site
---

When several people in the same lobby were running the companion app, the
site sometimes listed that lobby two or three times — once per person,
because their views of who was in it differed by a few seconds. The status
bar now shows the one lobby everyone is actually sitting in. Games that
genuinely run at the same time are still listed separately.
