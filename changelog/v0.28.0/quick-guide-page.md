area: wiki

There is now a **Quick guide** page — a first game drawn as one diagram: the
mode vote, the two classes you start with, whether to head for the city or
follow the squad, and the moment your weapon jams. Beside it, the AO with the
landing zone and Thalim marked, so those two names mean something.

Underneath, the detail:

- The jam drill — Immediate/Remedial Action, how often it takes the long way
  round, what each version costs and what shortens it — from the map's own
  numbers, with the button you click shown next to the hotkeys.
- Fire teams: the FT button, shared vision, the team leader's objective rally
  points, and what a Platoon Leader's Linked Fire Teams adds.

Send it to anyone who has just picked up the map.
