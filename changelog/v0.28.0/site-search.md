---
title: Search the whole site from anywhere
type: feature
area: site
impact: major
---
Press **Ctrl+F** (or **Ctrl+K**, or just **/**) anywhere on the site and a search
box opens over the page. Type a few letters and it offers matching units, items
and undead, MOS classes, Skill Identifiers, players, and the site's own pages —
arrow keys to move, Enter to open, Esc to close.

On a phone or tablet, the magnifier in the top bar opens the same thing.

It searches by the in-game name and by the map's internal id, so both
`AMX S-880` and `SiegeTank` find the tank, and class codes like `LK19` work too.
