---
title: Game times now say when the game was played
type: fix
area: replays
---
A replay's timestamp is the moment StarCraft saved the file, which is when you
left the game — so a game you walked away from was listed at the time you
finally closed StarCraft, hours after you played it. The last-games widget
showed one such game as "3 hours ago" when it had been played the previous
evening.

The replay list, the last-games widget and your profile's game history now all
date a game from when it started, as its own page already did.
