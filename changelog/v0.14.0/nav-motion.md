---
title: The menu button folds instead of flipping
type: improvement
area: site
impact: minor
---
The burger button is now one mark that folds into a cross and turns as the
menu opens, and unfolds on the way back — at every screen size, so the button
always shows what your next click does. The menu's edge glow, which tells you
there is more of the list above or below, fades in and out with it instead of
snapping on.
