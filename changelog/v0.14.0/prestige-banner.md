The overview now has a right column, the one class pages and player profiles
already use. **Prestiged** heads it in gold — everyone who prestiged in the
last 7 days, with their portrait and the level they jumped to — and **What's
new** sits under it. On a phone that column comes first, so the week's news is
what you land on.
