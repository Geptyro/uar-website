title: Vehicle gear unlocks now read from the map
type: fix
area: wiki
---
The [Predator](/mos/AssaultEngineer/predator) Floodlights and the [Alligator LK19](/mos/AlligatorLK19) Enhanced Weaponry showed no requirement, as if you got them for free. Both need a win on Hard or above with Outbreak.

The gear lists for the Walker, LK19, Predator, Robot and Combat Medic visor are now built from the map itself, so the requirements match what the game checks at the end of a game. Most of them accept any harder mode, and the Nightmare ones also count Apocalypse. Improved Rockets V never needed Outbreak. Cluster Rockets also need you to not die. The Walker's Flare Gun takes 500 kills with Outbreak, and the Robot needs to see 7 Strike Walks and 4 Focus Fires up close. Names and descriptions now come from the in-game buttons.
