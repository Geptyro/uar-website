---
title: Profiles are split into tabs
type: improvement
area: players
---
A player profile was one very long page. It is now four: **Overview** for ranks
and what they play, **Activity** for what they have earned, **Collection** for
medals, camouflages, decals, Skill Identifiers and vehicle gear, and
**Replays** for every game they appear in.

The replay table finally gets the room it wanted — it fills the window and
scrolls on its own, instead of being squeezed into a box at the bottom of the
page. Each tab also loads only its own data, so opening a profile is quicker
than it was.

Press **A** and **E** to move between the tabs, or the number keys to jump
straight to one.
