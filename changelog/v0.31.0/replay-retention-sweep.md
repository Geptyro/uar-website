---
title: Replay files are released once everyone has a newer game
type: improvement
area: replays
---
A replay file is your bank backup: if you lose your UAR progression, you
download your most recent game and recover it from there. Once every player in
an older game has a newer replay of their own, that file has stopped being
anyone's backup — so it is now released, automatically, instead of being kept
forever.

Nothing about the game is lost. It keeps its page, its players, its mode, its
result and its place in your history — only the downloadable file goes, and
those rows are marked "not stored" on the [replay list](/replays).
