---
title: Player and clan pages are instant on a second look
type: improvement
area: players
---
Player profiles, clan pages and the per-class top-ten tables were fetched from
the database from scratch on every single visit — so going back to a profile,
or just refreshing it, was as slow as the first time.

They are now remembered for a short while like the rest of the site, so a
revisit is instant and only genuinely new data is fetched.
