---
title: A steadier sidebar, and bigger class portraits in it
type: improvement
area: site
---
Opening and closing the sidebar used to shuffle everything in it — the icons
changed size, slid sideways and the rows shifted under them. Now only the panel's
width moves: the icons stay exactly where they are and the labels slide away
behind them.

The icons also line up with the menu button above them, and class portraits are
larger square tiles that fill their row, with a ring around the class you are
looking at.
