---
title: Every unit and class link now unfurls with its own card
type: improvement
area: site
---
Paste a unit or class page into Discord and the preview shows that unit: its
portrait, what it is, its in-game description and its headline stats — instead
of the one generic site card every link used to share.

Names that came out of the map with leftover text formatting in them (the
Outlaw deployment phantom was the worst offender) now read as plain words on the
page, in the browser tab and on the card.
