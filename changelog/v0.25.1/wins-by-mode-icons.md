---
title: Wins by mode now carries the mode icons
type: improvement
area: players
---
The **Wins by mode** board on your profile now shows each mode's icon and
colour, the same ones the replay lists use, and draws each bar in that mode's
colour instead of plain green. Your record reads as the same difficulty ramp
as your game history — where your wins sit on the green-to-red scale is
visible without reading a single row.
