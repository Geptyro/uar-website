---
title: Banned accounts no longer sit on the boards
type: improvement
area: players
---
The map keeps its own ban list, and wipes the experience of the accounts on it
every time they play. Those accounts had still been ranking against everyone
else here — one of them at the top of the career board with more than three
times the experience of the player below it.

They are now left off the leaderboards, the clan tables, the per-class playtime
boards, the weekly gains and the "played with" lists. Nothing is hidden: they
still appear in the games they actually played, searching still finds them, and
their profile still loads — under a red banner naming the ban and saying what
the map takes off them for it.
