---
title: Battle.net sign-in with linked player profiles
type: feature
area: players
---

You can now sign in with your Battle.net account (button in the top-right
corner) to link your StarCraft II profiles to their UAR player pages. Linked
profiles get a ✓ verified battletag badge, and your SC2 portrait shows up in
the top bar, on your player page, in the players table, and in the
leaderboards on the overview and class pages. Players who haven't linked get a
neutral placeholder portrait. You can refresh or remove the link any time from
the account page (cog icon next to your name).
