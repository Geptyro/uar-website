---
title: Games now show whether they were won or lost
type: feature
area: replays
---
Every game in the replay list, on a replay's page and in a player's replay
history now carries a green check for a win or a red cross for a loss, next to
how long the game ran.

Two things settle it. A replay that recorded the end of the game says so
directly. For everything else — a leaver's copy, and every game already in the
archive — the answer comes from your save file: the game counts your wins, and
your next uploaded game reveals whether the previous one was one. So a fresh
upload may show no result until somebody who was in it plays again.
