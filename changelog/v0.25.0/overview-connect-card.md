---
title: Sign-in card on the overview page
type: feature
area: site
---
If you are not signed in, the overview page now opens with a short card
explaining what a Battle.net link gets you: your StarCraft II profile linked to
your UAR player page, that page one click away from anywhere on the site, and
the "ready to play" flag in the top bar. Connecting takes one click from there —
Blizzard only shares your battletag and profile list, never a password.
