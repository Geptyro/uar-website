---
title: Played with joins the profile boards, replay history goes full width
type: improvement
area: players
---
**Played with** has moved off the sidebar and onto the profile itself, under
**Wins by mode** and beside **Classes played**. It now reads as the same kind
of board as those two — player, time together, bar — instead of a numbered
list.

**Replay history** now runs the full width of the page rather than the content
column, so the eleven columns of a game row have room to breathe.
