---
title: Base-game units get their names, icons and models
type: data
area: wiki
---
Units the map borrows from StarCraft II without renaming (the MULE, Rory Swann, the Crazyhorse APT delivery truck, the Overmind, the Civilians, Nova, Stukov) were empty pages. Their names and descriptions now come from Blizzard's own files, 60 more units have an icon, and the MULE, Rory Swann, Ares, the Overmind, the Crazyhorse APT, the sentry guns and the Field Camera have 3D models. Reskinned undead such as Cacumen, Creeper and Darreus get an icon rendered from their own model instead of the Zergling button they inherit in the editor. The M1 Sentry Gun shows all three of its looks, MMG/.50 Cal, flamethrower and poison, with a picker on its 3D card.
