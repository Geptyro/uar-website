---
title: Weapon damage explained, no more "?"
type: improvement
area: wiki
---
Weapon tables on class and entity pages now read the whole effect tree behind a shot. The 55 weapons that showed "?" for damage (the shotgun, carbine and machine pistol, every undead claw, Swann's grenade, the Goliath gun) have their number, and a notes column says what else the shot does: the random roll on top, armor it ignores, bonus against an attribute, splash rings as a share of the damage within a radius, extra hits, and damage on other branches such as an upgrade's rounds. Branches keyed on who fires (the machine gun's rounds per class) are settled per unit, so a Cyborg's gun reads as a Cyborg's gun.
