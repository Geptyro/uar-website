title: A placed thing and the crate it came in are one entity
type: improvement
area: wiki
---
The map builds every deployable twice: the crate you carry and the thing it becomes once placed, a Claymore2 that turns into a Claymore, a packed sentry gun that is set up as the MK44. The site now reads that link from the data and the script and shows one entity per thing: the deployed one, with the crate's item facts on it and a "Carried as" line. The crate ids still resolve, they land on the merged page.
